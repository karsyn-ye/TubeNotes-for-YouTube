# TubeNotes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-12-09

TubeNotes runs locally in your browser to help you capture YouTube timestamps, transcripts, notes, and optional audio snippets for your personal use. We do not operate backend servers.

## Data we handle
- Video context: The current YouTube page URL, video ID, and title so notes attach to the correct video.
- User-generated content: Notes you type, pinned timestamps, transcripts we read from the page, and optional short audio recordings you capture.
- Local-only storage: These data are stored in the browser’s `chrome.storage.local`. Nothing is sent to external servers by the extension.

## Data sharing
- No sale or sharing. We do not send data to third parties or our own servers.
- Removal: Removing the extension or clearing its data deletes stored notes/pins.

## Permissions use
- `storage` / `unlimitedStorage`: Save your notes, pins, transcripts, and clips locally without size surprises.
- `activeTab` and host permission (`https://www.youtube.com/*`): Identify the current YouTube video to attach notes correctly.
- `scripting`: Inject UI and read page elements (e.g., transcript and timestamps) on YouTube.

## Security
- All processing happens locally in your browser. No remote code is loaded.
