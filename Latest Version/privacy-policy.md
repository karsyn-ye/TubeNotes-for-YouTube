# TubeNotes Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2025-12-29

TubeNotes helps you capture YouTube timestamps, transcripts, notes, and media for your personal use. We prioritize your privacy and store your personal content locally.

## Data We Collect

### 1. Account Information
*   **Email Address**: We collect your email address via **Google Firebase Authentication** to create your account.
*   **Communications**: We may occasionally send you emails about new features, important product updates, or service changes. You may opt out of these non-essential communications at any time by following the unsubscribe link in the email or contacting us.

### 2. Usage Data (Analytics)
We use **Google Analytics 4** to understand how the extension is used and to improve the product. We track:
*   **Feature Usage**: Actions such as pinning screenshots, recording video clips, saving notes, and exporting data.
*   **Session Data**: Duration of use and basic interaction metrics.
*   This data is associated with a pseudonymous Client ID and is **not** linked to the content of your notes.

### 3. Local Content (Private)
The following data is stored **locally** in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) and is **never** sent to our servers or analytics:
*   The text of your notes.
*   Captured screenshots and video clips.
*   Audio recordings.
*   Specific YouTube video titles/IDs associated with your notes.

## Service Providers

We use the following trusted third-party services:
*   **Google Firebase**: For secure authentication and account management.
*   **Google Analytics**: For anonymous product usage tracking.

## Data Sharing
*   **No Sale**: We do not sell your data to third parties.
*   **No Ad Tracking**: We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

## User Rights & Deletion
*   **Account Deletion**: You can request account deletion by contacting us.
*   **Local Data**: Uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser data will immediately delete all locally stored notes and media.
